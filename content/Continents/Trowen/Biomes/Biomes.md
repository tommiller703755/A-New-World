Trowen is a cold and lush continent, dotted with sparse settlements, especially towards the East. Trowen is primarily made up of three biomes. East Trowen is split between temperate forests in the North, making up the vast majority of the [[Bethionese Theocracy]] and about half of the [[Trag Empire]], and unusually cold jungles in the south, taking up the entirety of Khret and most of [[Nasesh]]. West Trowen however is dominated by a giant, and seemingly unnatural, tundra. The upper half of the tundra is controlled by the empire, with the lower half being controlled by the [[Republic of Vun]]. No one knows why the tundra formed, though there's no shortage of speculation. 

# Forests
The temperate forests of Western Trowen are home to many bizarre arcane sites, frequently blamed, sometimes even correctly, on the many rogue and wandering wizards who use the regions seclusion to conduct bizarre and unethical experiments without interference. In addition to these wizards multiple druidic sects call the region home. These sects are highly isolationist, frequently being unknown and all but forgotten by the world for decades, until loggers choose to chop for wood in the wrong forest. These sects and their beliefs vary wildly, some venerating the balance and harmony of nature, others the savageness of predators. The largest sect, which has multiple settlements through the Bethionese Theocracy venerate [[Iteus]] and are widely feared, despite following the same deity as the theocracy. Gentler sects do exist though, with some even opposing the theocracy's brutal treatment of heretics.
### Features and wonders
- A stretch of territory said to be home to an immortal silver stag that will grant those that catch it a wish.
- A long abandoned cemetery rumored to be haunted, ghastly wails echoing through the trees for miles around.
- A pine grove made up of multiple concentric rings of trees, at the center of which is a faerie ring that serves as a portal to the feywild.
- A doorway made of twisted vines that hum with latent magic.
- An antlered skull the size of a building.
- Centered in a clearing dominated by giant purple stalks of vines stands a jet black obelisk inscribed with jagged markings.
- A large, ring-shaped tower with no windows or doors. In the center of the ring grows a pure white elm tree.
- A spring of lilac-scented fluorescent purple water that bubbles out of the massive stump of an ancient tree.
- A seemingly abandoned city of strange spherical buildings built on the cast caps of colossal vibrant blue mushrooms.
- A rock formation that looks like a hag. It spawns in a different region of the forest every dawn.
- A tree-filled grotto that surrounds a pool where the shed skin of a divine is said to lie.

# Jungle
Southern Trowen is home to a truly strange biome, a snowy jungle. Despite the frigid temperatures plants thrive, even those that typically require far warmer temperatures, leading to this region producing most of the continents food. This is largely accepted as an effect of the Flux-wood trees that are only known to grow in this region, mainly in Khret. Plants and animals in this region have been known to spontaneously mutate, usually in beneficial ways, though sometimes in random horrific ways. As a result these jungles are home to life far stranger and more varied than most of the rest of the planet.
### Features and wonders
- An overgrown temple ruin rumored to have a degree of sentience.
- A path that stretches that stretches for miles, lined in faerie lights, that leads to a glade tended by am awakened tree.
- An invisible city of the fey high in the canopy.

# Tundra
The tundra that dominates western Trowen is without a doubt the continents greatest mystery, and one that scholars have debated for centuries. Based on it's position on the planet it should be somewhat cold but nowhere near what it is. Over the years hundreds of ideas have been put forth, ranging from it being the result of a wizard circles experiment gone wrong, the divines sealing something beneath the layers of permafrost, and the home of the goddess of ice and snow, *Name Needed* being hidden somewhere within the vast expanse.
### Features and wonders
- A hot spring in the middle of a snowy plain where strange, possibly fey, creatures have been seen.
- Massive pale yellow crystals stick out of a frozen mountain. Inside the crystals are massive shadowy shapes that sometimes move.
- Giant glowing columns of ice stretch high above the landscape. Roaring blue flames burn at the top of each column.
- Inside a ring of standing stones, on a frozen plateau, it is always a warm spring day.
- An area of the rocky tundra covered with bright pink permafrost. Using this frost in a potion increases its potency.
- A large chasm that spews frigid air and icy water in a large plume 100 feet high.
- Frozen into the side of a mountain is a sphere that appears to contain a living aurora borealis.
