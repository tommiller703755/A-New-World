#### Ravkin Traits:

**Age:** Ravkin mature at about the same rate as humans for the first 45-50 years of their lives, before their aging slows to a crawl, allowing them to live between 150 and 200 years.
**Size:** Ravkin are typically below four feet tall.
**Heightened Senses:** Ravkin have very sensitive hearing and sight, allowing them to both notice small prey at considerable distance, and notice details that others would typically miss (proficiency with perception and investigation).
**Carnivorous Nature:** Ravkin are obligate carnivores. They don't eat nearly as much as humans, and typically only need to eat once a day, although they'll often eat twice a day, once at dawn and once at sunset, when they can.
