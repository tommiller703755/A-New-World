### Introduction
Fulgur are an ancient race of constructs, originally created as slaves by the Hecatoncheires, eight years ago they joined the [[Vantari]] in overthrowing their masters and taking their freedom. Because they've only just recently achieved freedom they don't have a strong culture of their own, but have a deep yearning to experience the world they'd been denied access to for so long. While some of them remained in the keeps they seized from the Hecatoncheires, most traveled out into the broader world, wandering until they eventually find a place they can settle down in and call home. 
### Biology
Despite it being a bit of a misnomer, the fulgur possess a very unique "biology." 
- The bodies fulgur possess are little more than puppets, with a fulgur's "true self" being a small, incredibly intricate device of concentric rotating rings build around a glowing crystalline core. This core, which can fit comfortably in the palm of a humans hand, is essentially the "soul" of the fulgur, and can be placed into a wide array of bodies.
	- These bodies can vary greatly, while most maintain a basic humanoid shape, other's can be completely alien. Some of the most common of these alien ones are ones that are completely stationary, such as forges, where  a fulgur blacksmith can literally become one with their forge.
- The relationship fulgur have with their bodies, or vessels, as they're frequently called, is inconsistent, with some growing deeply attached to their vessels, caring for them and maintaining them over centuries, while others view the vessel almost like clothes or a tool, changing bodies as the situation demands it.
- The knowledge needed to create or even repair a fulgur's "true self," has been lost for centuries, when the Hecatoncheires who originally created them were killing in a lengthy war with the [[Trag Empire]]. As a result the fulgur have been slowly going extinct ever since, and will likely completely cease to exist if the methods used to make them cannot somehow be recovered.
### Culture
The fulgur don't have a particularly unified culture, having been literally created to serve as tools for the Hecatoncheires. As a result those that chose to remain in the fortresses and keeps taken from the Hecatoncheires have largely been folded in by the vantari, while those that left and began wandering the world are in a continual process of picking up elements from the cultures they encounter, adopting them
****
#### Fulgur Traits:
**Age:** The fulgur, because of their mechanical nature, don't age. Their bodies wear down, and can last anywhere from a few years to a few centuries depending on construction and maintenance, but the core never "ages."
**Varying forms:** Every fulgur is highly unique, and may be especially well suited to some tasks while being worse at others.
**Craftsmen:** All fulgur are experts in at least one craft, with most of them having served as craftsmen for their former masters.
**Disease and Poison Resistant:** Because of their mechanical bodies fulgur are immune to disease and poison.
