### Introduction
Gaunts, as the name implies, are lanky humanoids, their rail thin-stature emphasized by their height, standing a full head taller than most humans. Gaunts are widely known for their unsettling appearance, and close ties to Vipkila's three moons, with it often being said that the moons themselves sent them down to serve as their representatives. There is more truth to this idea than most realize, although not even the gaunts themselves can agree on all the details.
### Biology
Gaunts, in addition to being lanky, appear to be blessed with a degree of grace and fluid movement that verges on unnatural, and can often be unsettling to look at. This fact is only made worse by how pronounced their joints are. Adding to this unsettling appearance is the fact they they're skin ranges from an ash gray to pale ivory, and that they're naturally completely bald. These traits together have made gaunts draw frequent comparisons to bodies in a morgue. Finally their eyes are universally a pale silver, and far larger than those of an ordinary human.
The most interesting thing about gaunts though is how they seem to draw strength from the moon they're associated with. This comes with upsides during a full moon, but can be debilitating during a new moon, causing many gaunts to go into seclusion when their moon is new. The exact effects vary based on the gaunt's associated moon, but it can be generally described:
- <b><u>New Moon:</u></b> The gaunt grows weaker and more withdrawn. Their voice during this stage will begin to sound raspy, and they may struggle with both focus and memory. Most gaunts will try to isolate themselves during this period to ride it out, trusting their care to gaunts associated with other moons.
-  <b><u>Waxing Moon:</u></b> Gaunts gradually return to their ordinary amount of vitality, and are often overtaken by a feeling of wanderlust, although they don't travel far.
-  <b><u>Full Moon:</u></b> This is when gaunts are at their strongest and most lucid, their eyes shining far brighter than usual. #### Gaunt Traits:

**Age:** Gaunts have around the same lifespan as humans.
**Size:** Gaunts are rail thin and typically stand a head taller than humans.
**Gliding:** Their transformation from butterflies took their ability to fly, but lepids still possess the ability to glide tremendous distances. Their settlements often feature a center spire they can glide from when traveling abroad, to give them a good start.
**Eerie:** Lepids are often ill received by other races, especially those that know little of them. Their alien appearance, and the strange buzzing their means of speaking produce puts others on guard.
**Frail:** Lepids are notably frailer than most races, being weaker in terms of strength, and possessing exoskeletons that easily shatter.
**Disease Resistant:** As a result of the nectar within them lepids can resist all known natural and magical illnesses.
