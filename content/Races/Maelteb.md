### Introduction

The maelteb are those that have had their vary natures changed by encounters with the fey. Most maelteb are born fey touched, although it often doesn't show signs until their teens. While it's far rarer it's still possible for someone to be turned into a maelteb later in life, although such a feat requires a truly extraordinary event, most commonly the direct attention of an arch-fey. Maelteb are poorly understood by the general populace and often forced to flee from their homes when the changes start. Most scholars agree that the myth of the changeling comes from the maelteb, as many people fail to realize that their child or neighbor has been changed, instead believing that something else is impersonating them.
### Biology

Maelteb are highly variable, with their exact form being influenced on what type of fey affected them. For example those who were affected by a dryad or similar nature spirit may have leathery bark-like skin, while those affected by a nymph will begin to grow scales and share their propensity for water. The most common form of maelteb by far however are those that are part satyr, mostly to to satyr's willingness to bed just about anything with a pulse. These maelteb share the satyr's goat-like hair, with it often being an early sign that one is a maelteb. The horns they grow differ from those of true satyr's as they're much closer to deer's horns than goats, and like deer are shed and regrown annually.
- All Maelteb are technically considered fey, and as such anything that wards away fey also wards away them. This only occurs after the change starts, which sometimes leads to people being warded out of their very own homes, or worse, imprisoned by wards they themselves helped setup.
- If two maelteb have a child together the child is always another maelteb, while if a maelteb has a child with a member of another race the child may simply be a member of that other race.
- The bodies of maelteb are naturally suited to wielding magic, making them natural spell casters. While not all of them hone this ability those that do have the potential to wield vast power. It's speculated that the ritual the arch-mages of [[Nasesh]] go through is effectively turning them into maelteb, although there's definitely some other component going on there.
	- Unfortunately this fact can often times make their lives even more difficult, as it means that their bodies are sought after by various dark wizards, who aim to take advantage of this property for their own ends.
### Culture
Maelteb are often outcast from society, and are as a result highly distrustful of it. While some find homes in arcane orders, or join enclaves in larger cities where their strangeness is more likely to be tolerated, the majority abandon society altogether, either becoming lone hermits or forming small villages, hidden from the broader nations they inhabit. The more established villages often monitor nearby settlements, intervening if a maelteb is born there and doing their best to prevent harm from coming to them. Unfortunately this is often misconstrued  as them "whisking away," the child, and has led to the destruction of some maelteb townships.
One township located in the [[Trag Empire]], Agaptes' rest, has become large enough that it's managed to step into the light, acting more as a city state than a small, hidden village. This is mainly due to a trio of powerful maelteb wizards who call the city home and have come forth as it's protectors. This is the only major settlement available to the maelteb, and many have begun long pilgrimages to get there, although the Empire's recent aggressiveness has made the people of the city nervous.

#### Maelteb Traits:

**Age:** Maelteb have the same lifespans as humans.
**Size:** Maelteb vary in size far more than humans, typically standing either far taller or shorter than humans, depending on their exact nature.
**Fey-Ancestry:** Anything that would work on a fey works on the maelteb. This can sometimes be beneficial as it means they share the fey's resistance to mind-altering magic, but can also be a severe detriment, as they share the fey's aversion to iron.
